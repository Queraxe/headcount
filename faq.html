How to take attendance in Google Meet

A free way to record who attended your Google Meet call, when they joined and left, and to export the list to Excel or CSV. Works with any Google account.

Add Headcount to Chrome – free

Step by step

  1. Install Headcount from the Chrome Web Store and pin it to your toolbar.
  2. Join your Google Meet call as usual. Attendance is recorded automatically, there is no button to press.
  3. Open the People panel in Meet in larger calls. Meet doesn't show every video tile, but the People panel lists everyone, so Headcount sees all participants.
  4. Click the Headcount icon to see who is in the call right now, or open a past meeting.
  5. Click "Export CSV" to download the attendance list for Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get Google Meet attendance without Business Plus or Education Plus?

Yes. Google's built-in attendance reports are only available in some paid Google Workspace editions. Headcount works with any Google account, including free Gmail, Business Starter, Business Standard and Education Fundamentals.

How do I export the Google Meet participant list to Excel or CSV?

Open Headcount, select the meeting and click Export CSV. The file opens directly in Excel, Numbers and Google Sheets, with names, join time, leave time and minutes per person.

Can I see when each participant joined and left?

Yes. Headcount shows the first join time, last leave time and total minutes for every participant. People who drop out and rejoin are counted correctly, and anyone who joins more than 5 minutes after the start is marked as late.

Do I need to start recording before the meeting?

No. Headcount starts on its own when you join a call and saves every meeting to its history.

Is it free?

Yes. Headcount is free, with no account and no sign-up.

Does Headcount store or send my meeting data?

No data leaves your browser. Attendance lists are stored locally in Chrome on your computer. There are no servers, no tracking and no ads. You can delete single meetings at any time, and uninstalling removes everything. Details in the privacy policy.

Who is it for?

Teachers taking class attendance, tutors, trainers, coaches, clubs, HR teams, and anyone who needs a record of who attended an online meeting.

Do participants need to install anything?

No. Only the person who wants the attendance list needs Headcount. If you record other people's attendance, let them know, as your local privacy rules may require.

Does it work in other browsers?

Headcount is made for Google Chrome. It may also work in other Chromium browsers such as Edge or Brave that install extensions from the Chrome Web Store.
